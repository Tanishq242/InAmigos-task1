About us

InAmigos Foundation, founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization committed to creating lasting social impact. With its base in Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates across India, addressing critical societal issues through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers.

At InAmigos Foundation, we believe in the power of collective action. Our transparent operations and strong partnerships enable us to bring real, measurable change to society. Through our social media presence and online platforms, we ensure that every initiative reaches the right audience, encouraging more individuals and organizations to join our mission.

Our projects

Discover the initiatives driving change across communities. Each project is designed to tackle specific social and environmental challenges with measurable impact.

Project Seva: Distribution of Food and Clothing

Tackling hunger and inadequate clothing among impoverished communities, Project Seva provides nutritious meals and essential clothing. Beyond meeting basic needs, it fosters dignity and raises awareness about food security, ensuring that families don’t go to bed hungry or suffer in extreme weather conditions.

Project Bachpanshala: Education for Children

Education is a powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty. Project Bachpanshala offers children access to quality education, arranges learning sessions, and provides school supplies. The initiative inspires curiosity and encourages knowledge, ensuring every child has the opportunity to learn and grow.

Project Jeev: Feeding and Protecting Animals

Project Jeev focuses on the welfare of stray and abandoned animals, providing food, medical care, and safe shelter. The program raises awareness of animal rights and advocates for humane treatment, promoting a society that values compassion toward all living beings.

Project Udaan: Empowerment of Women

A just society begins with empowered women. Project Udaan supports women through awareness campaigns, skill development workshops, and entrepreneurial opportunities. By fostering confidence and financial independence, this program transforms lives and strengthens communities.

Project Prakriti: Environmental Protection

Focused on sustainability, Project Prakriti organizes tree-planting drives, clean-up campaigns, and conservation workshops. It involves local communities to instill a sense of environmental responsibility, ensuring a healthier planet for future generations.
